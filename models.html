Physics models

Three models of the cosmos, each one you can run, slide and take apart in the browser.

Each model turns its equations into something you can watch: named states, live equations and the observations that test it. They run here in any modern browser with nothing to install, and each one is also part of its theory's repository, where Physics IDE can open it from the file tree.

Aeon Horizon: the ΛCDM model of the universe

Slide from 10⁻³⁶ seconds to 10¹¹⁰ years. The universe grows from a dot through inflation, the quark–gluon plasma and the opaque fog to the real cosmic microwave background (COBE, WMAP or Planck) and real Hubble Ultra Deep Field galaxies, then on into the accelerating future. Feynman-diagram insets show what each early era is doing, a chart compares Planck's acoustic peaks with ΛCDM, and a timeline marks each prediction as verified, partly verified or untested.

StringScope: vibrating string states

An oscilloscope for vibrating strings, styled after the HP 1205B. Switch between the bosonic string in 26 dimensions, Type IIA/IIB superstrings in 10 and M-theory in 11. Step the oscillator quanta, momentum, winding and radius, and watch the string vibrate in 3-D while the mode expansion, mass formula and level matching update live. The DISPLAY knob picks the channels: displacement traces, worldsheet fermions and the spinor internal space, and the M2 and M5 branes. Each state is named, from the tachyon to the graviton, and T-duality is one click.

Launch in browser Source

Almagest Visualizer: the Ptolemaic model in motion

The Almagest in motion, with Ptolemy's own parameters and epoch: deferents, epicycles, equants, Mercury's moving centre and the lunar crank. Run it at 1:1 or years per second to watch the planets trace their retrograde loops, jump to any date, and see how far the model has drifted from the real sky since 140 AD. Tilt it into a 3-D sphere of his 1,028 catalogued stars, with precession, the Book XIII planet latitudes and his own description of each star, and open his tables to copy or download.
