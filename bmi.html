BMI theory

A braneworld cosmology developed in the open: where version 1 failed and where version 2 stands.

Research in progress. BMI is not an established or finished theory. Its first version failed, and this page shows that record as openly as the progress. Nothing here is a claim of discovery.

BMI is a byproduct of a broader study of working with AI on theoretical physics, and it remains in use as a stress test for Physics IDE: a measure of how well the IDE detects flaws and comprehends an entire project.

Version 1: where it failed

The original manuscript as audited: the first hard stop at its first equation, the names added without definitions, the claimed derivations and validations, and the net ledger of 21 constants, 14 free functions and no derived number. Critical failures are haloed in red, contradictions are joined by ≠, and each box names the trap it fell into.

Version 2: from the axiom to the data

The rebuild, from the axiom through the action, the effective theory and the nine parameters to the predictions and the data that test them. Each causal link carries a ✓ when it is verified and a ? when it is not yet worked out; results below a ? are drawn as conditional. Every box links to its file and check in the repository, and the comparison view pairs each v1 failure with the rule in v2 that guards against it.
