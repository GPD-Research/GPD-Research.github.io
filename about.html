Gregory Phillip Dearth

Independent scientist, researcher and software developer in Virginia.

I'm Gregory Phillip Dearth, and GPD Research is where I publish my work. I build applications and physics models, using AI as a collaborative tool, and release them as open source on GitHub.

Work

  • Physics IDE: an AI-assisted Linux workspace for developing and testing theoretical physics frameworks.
  • BMI theory: a braneworld cosmology in development, tested against gravitational-wave and DESI data.
  • Physics models: Aeon Horizon (the ΛCDM model of the universe), StringScope (string theory) and the Almagest Visualizer (the Ptolemaic system), all running in the browser.
  • Hyper Browser: a dual-pane file manager for Android with Google Drive as a pane.
  • Magnus: a highway incident-scene illustrator for Safety Service Patrol training.
  • SimpleStax: guided calibration and stacking for astrophotography on Linux.
